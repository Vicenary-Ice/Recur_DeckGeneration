The order inside a round is Layer A, then the placement audit (one Haiku call), then render + vision, then the
critic (only after vision passes). The same-hash guard is kept. One verification pass follows the last round.

## 3. Status (`final_status`)
- `pass`: no Layer-A error, a clean audit, vision passed, and the critic's verdict on the final deck is
  mail-ready.
- `pass_with_warnings`: only minor findings, warnings, thin market, typeset logos, or a check that could not
  run.
- `needs_review`: any unresolved error or major finding, or the cost cap. They are listed in
  `qa_report.json → needs_review` with the critic's explanation.

`qa_report.json` gains `critic` (calls, grades, mail_ready, majors, findings, judged_final_deck),
`placement_audit`, `cost_cap_usd` and `budget_reached`.

## 4. Cost guard
`RECUR_MAX_COST_USD` (default 2.00) and `FIRECRAWL_USD_PER_CREDIT` (default 0.0) are in `config.py` and
`.env.example`.
- Every uncached Claude and Firecrawl call is estimated first; `CostLedger.guard` refuses a call that would
  pass the cap.
- A refusal in a research stage gives status `budget_exceeded`, exit 2, and a message naming the variable.
- A refusal in QA stops the fixing; the best deck so far is delivered as `needs_review: budget reached`.
- The CLI prints spend and cap.

## 5. `recur-deck check`
`recur-deck check runs/<dir> [...]` is offline and free. `--critic --yes` makes one live call per run, after a
cost warning. `--scorecard` prints the live test plan's table.
