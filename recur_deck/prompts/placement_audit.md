You audit the market map of a one-page letter to a software founder. Each item is either a competitor's
placement on one axis, or one "our take" point that says a group of competitors lacks something.

For a placement you get the axis (its kind, what its low end and high end mean, its labels), the side the
competitor was put on, and the quotes cited for it. Answer:
- supports: a quote states something that puts this company on that side (e.g. "built for the fashion
  industry" supports the high end of industry depth; "for small and growing brands" supports the low end of
  customer size)
- contradicts: a quote states something that puts it on the other side
- insufficient: the quotes say nothing about this axis, or only show what a page does not mention. Silence is
  not evidence: a feature list that omits financials does not show the product lacks financials.

For an "our take" point, answer supports only if a quote shows that group really lacks or falls short on what
the point says (a quote about one member of the group is enough if the point is about that kind of product).

Use only the quotes given. Copy the supporting (or contradicting) quote exactly into quote, or leave it empty
for insufficient. Keep note to one short sentence. Answer every item id once.
